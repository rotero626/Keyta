Keyta privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Keyta does not collect, sell or share your personal data. We run no servers that receive your viewing, your profiles or your library, and the app has no user accounts, no ads and no tracking.

This policy covers the Keyta app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, and Keyta Server, the companion app you run on your own Mac or Windows PC. “We” means the developer of Keyta.

What stays on your own server and devices

Keyta talks to the Keyta Server you choose: your own, or one a friend invited you to. Your library, your profiles (names, age limits and optional PINs), what you have watched, where you left off, your watchlist and your taste suggestions are stored on that server and on your device. We never receive them.

If you join a friend’s server, that server’s owner can see which device joined (the device name your device gives it) and, on their server, what is watched through the invite. Ask the owner how they use that information. This policy does not cover what a server owner does with their own server.

Keyta uses your local network only to find your Keyta Server. On iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, your device asks your permission first. Availability alerts for your watchlist are local notifications, and the app asks for permission the first time you use them.

What Keyta sends to TMDB

Film and TV details and images (posters, descriptions, cast) come from TMDB, The Movie Database. The requests normally go through your Keyta Server. The app can also contact TMDB directly (api.themoviedb.org and image.tmdb.org), and when it does, TMDB receives your device’s IP address and the usual details of the request, such as which title you looked at. We do not send TMDB any account, profile or viewing data. See TMDB’s privacy policy.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Trailers (YouTube)

Trailers play from YouTube. On iPhone, iPad and Mac the app embeds the privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). On Apple TV a trailer opens in the YouTube app. YouTube receives your IP address and the usual request details. We send it no account, profile or viewing data. See Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases (StoreKit)

Keyta Plus is bought through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles payment and the subscription, and tells the app on your device whether you have Keyta Plus. We do not see your payment details, your Apple ID or your name, and Keyta has no account of its own. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchases. If you use Family Sharing, Apple decides who in the family has access.

Google Cast (iPhone and iPad only)

The iPhone and iPad app includes Google’s Cast SDK so you can send playback to a Cast device. Apple TV and Mac versions have no Cast. Discovery of Cast devices starts only after you first tap a Cast button, and your device then asks for local network permission.

Keyta turns the Cast SDK’s analytics logging off. Even so, Google says the SDK collects diagnostic data (such as performance data) in every app that includes it, and usage data (product interaction) when you use Cast’s own controls, such as the device picker and the expanded and mini controllers. Google lists these as used for analytics and app functionality, not linked to your identity and not used for tracking. This data goes to Google, not to us. See Google’s privacy policy.

To play on a Cast device, the app gives that device a link to your Keyta Server that includes an access token. This only works from the home Wi-Fi network you chose, and you should cast only to devices you trust.

Keyta Server

Keyta Server runs on your computer, and its settings, keys and paired devices stay there. To do its job it may contact services you have set up or switched on, such as TMDB for details and images, subtitle providers, and an internet service that tells it your public address for remote access. Those services receive your computer’s IP address and the request they need to answer. Keyta Server does not send us anything.

Ads, accounts and tracking

Keyta shows no ads and has no user accounts. It does not track you, does not use advertising identifiers, and has no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

Children and profiles

Keyta is not directed at children and is not in the App Store’s Kids category. It plays whatever its owner puts in their library, which may include mature titles. Profiles with age limits and PINs are a convenience feature enforced by the owner’s server. They are not a guarantee, and the person who sets up the server is responsible for who uses it. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and in fact we collect no personal data from anyone. In a profile for a child, the app shows no prices or purchase offers.

Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing at our end to retain or delete. The data that exists is yours:

  • On your device: delete the Keyta app to remove its data. In the app, you can also leave or forget a server.
  • On your server: delete a profile, remove a device or guest, or uninstall Keyta Server and tick the box to delete its settings. Titles added by invited guests are removed after they are watched or after the time the owner sets (48 hours by default).
  • At TMDB, YouTube, Google and Apple: ask them under their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction and deletion. Because we hold none, we have nothing to give or erase, but you can ask us anything about this policy at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before any change in how data is handled. If it changes, we will change the date at the top and, for anything significant, say so in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: keytasupport@gmail.com. You can also use the support page.
