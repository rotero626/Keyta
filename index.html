Your own library, on your own server

Keyta turns the films and TV shows on your own computer into a simple, good-looking library you can watch on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

How it works

  1. Run Keyta Server, a free app, on the Mac or Windows PC that holds your media.
  2. Open Keyta on your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV. It finds your server on your home network.
  3. Browse, pick something and press play.

Keyta does not include or sell any films or shows. It plays what is on your server.

What you get

A home screen that knows you

Suggestions for what to watch next, drawn from your own library, with posters, descriptions and cast.

Profiles for the household

Everyone gets their own place, watch history and optional PIN, with age limits set on your server.

Pick up where you left off

Stop on one device and carry on from another.

Plays almost anything

Subtitles and smooth playback of most file types, on all four platforms.

Free at home, Keyta Plus for extras

Browsing and playing your own library on your home network is free, and stays free. This includes profiles and your watchlist.

Keyta Plus is an optional subscription that adds extras: playing when you are away from home, Live TV from a source you add to your own server, Google Cast from iPhone and iPad, inviting friends to your library, and two ways to add a title: Watch Now plays a title right away from your own server, without saving it, and Save has your server keep a copy in your library. Both happen on your own server, using services you set up there. See the subscription terms.
