Keyta Plus subscription

Keyta Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Using Keyta at home on your own network is free and does not need it.

What it addsPlaying away from home (including adaptive quality), Live TV from a source you add to your own server, Watch Now and Save, Google Cast on iPhone and iPad, and creating invites for friends. Watch Now plays a title right away from your own server, without saving it. Save has your server keep a copy in your library. Both happen on your own server, using services you set up there. Friends who join your server play your library at home or away; their own Plus features on their device need their own subscription.
Plans and lengthMonthly (renews every month) and yearly (renews every year).
PriceThe price is set by the App Store for your country and currency and is shown in the app before you subscribe. It is charged to your Apple account.
Free trialNew subscribers may get a 7-day free trial on either plan, if their Apple account has not used a Keyta Plus trial before. The app shows whether you are eligible. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it turns into a paid subscription.
RenewalThe subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period, at the price shown in the app at that time.
Cancel or manageManage or cancel any time in your Apple account settings: on iPhone or iPad, Settings, your name, Subscriptions; on Mac, the App Store, your account, Subscriptions; on Apple TV, Settings, Users and Accounts, your account, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it. If you cancel, you keep Keyta Plus until the end of the period you paid for. Any unused part of a free trial is lost when you buy a subscription.
RefundsPayments and refunds are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
RestoreIn Keyta, Settings, Keyta Plus, Restore Purchases. Family Sharing applies if you have switched it on for subscriptions.

Prices and trial offers may change. Your subscription price changes only with the notice Apple requires.
